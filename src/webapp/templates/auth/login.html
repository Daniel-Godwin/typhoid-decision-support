Clinical decision support for typhoid assessment

A Support Vector Machine model trained on 31,087 records, deployed as a point-of-care triage aid for settings where confirmatory testing is limited.

  • Calibrated probability with every prediction
  • Explains which findings drove each result
  • Full audit trail of every assessment
  • Sub-10 ms inference for real-time use

Research prototype. Decision support only — it does not replace blood culture, confirmatory testing or clinical judgement.
